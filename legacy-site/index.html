Holistic Education

Homeschooling in India

Empowering parents to take charge of their child's education with mindfulness, flexibility, and a holistic approach to learning.

✦ Homeschooling Is Legal in India ✦

The Right to Education (RTE) Act of 2009 requires education for children aged 6–14 but does not mandate school attendance. The Delhi High Court (2010) confirmed that parents are free to educate their children at home. The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 further supports alternative education pathways. No registration is required.

📋 Why Homeschool?

Homeschooling offers families in India the freedom to:

  • Personalise education to your child's unique learning style and pace
  • Integrate holistic wellbeing, meditation, and mindfulness into daily learning
  • Protect children from academic pressure and mental health challenges
  • Flexible scheduling around family life, travel, and extracurriculars
  • Choose from Indian (NIOS) and international (Cambridge, Edexcel) curricula
  • Stronger parent-child bonding through shared learning experiences

🎓 Certification Pathways

Homeschooled students in India have clear paths to recognised qualifications:

  • NIOS — Government board, equivalent to CBSE. Eligible for JEE, NEET, CLAT
  • Cambridge (IGCSE/A-Levels) — Recognised by 160+ countries, accepted by Indian universities
  • Pearson Edexcel — International GCSE/A-Levels, recognised in 80+ countries
  • CBSE/ICSE — Not available to private candidates (requires school enrolment)
  • NIOS is the most affordable option (under ₹5,000 for registration and exams)

📚 Curriculum Options

Indian families can choose from a growing range of curricula:

  • NIOS — Secondary (Class 10) & Senior Secondary (Class 12)
  • Cambridge International — IGCSE, AS & A-Levels
  • Pearson Edexcel — International GCSE & A-Levels
  • US K-12 — Cognia accredited, NCAA approved for athletes
  • Many families combine NIOS with international curricula for maximum flexibility

🏛️ University Admission

Homeschooled students can absolutely pursue higher education:

  • Indian Universities via NIOS: NIOS is recognised by Association of Indian Universities as equivalent to CBSE
  • Indian Universities via Cambridge/Edexcel: Most Indian universities accept international A-Levels
  • International Universities: Cambridge A-Levels accepted in 160+ countries
  • Competitive Exams: JEE, NEET, CLAT all accessible through NIOS
